About

Gregorian Group of Institutions

Gregorian Institute of Technology was founded in 2017 at Kangazha, Kottayam by the Mar Gregorious Memorial Education and Charitable Trust. GIT Engineering followed in 2023, approved by AICTE and affiliated with KTU.

Within seven years the group became the first technical institution in Kerala to achieve NBA accreditation in this timeframe — building a reputation for industry-ready skilling on a serene, eco-friendly campus.
